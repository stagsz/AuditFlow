## ISO 9001 self-assessment checklist for small teams

A practical checklist covering scope, interested parties, risk-based thinking, and operational controls for SMEs running internal audits.

### 1. Scope (Clause 4.3)
- [ ] Documented scope of the QMS is current.
- [ ] Scope covers products/services, locations, and exclusions with justification.
- [ ] Scope is communicated to relevant interested parties.

### 2. Interested parties (Clause 4.2)
- [ ] Relevant parties are identified and documented.
- [ ] Requirements are reviewed on a defined cadence.
- [ ] Monitoring plan includes regulatory, customer, and internal requirements.

### 3. Risk-based thinking (Clause 6.1)
- [ ] Risks and opportunities are identified for each clause/process.
- [ ] Actions are proportionate to impact.
- [ ] Residual risk is recorded and reviewed.

### 4. Operational controls (Clause 8.1)
- [ ] Documented information is available at the point of use.
- [ ] Process criteria are defined and controlled.
- [ ] Non-conformity handling is consistent with Clause 10.
