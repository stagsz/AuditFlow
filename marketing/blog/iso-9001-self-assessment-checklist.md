## ISO 9001 self-assessment checklist for small teams

A practical checklist covering scope, interested parties, risk-based thinking, and operational controls for SMEs running internal audits.

### Why run a self-assessment first?
Self-assessments let you find gaps before the external auditor does. They are low-cost, build internal ownership, and reduce the number of findings on the day. For small teams, the whole exercise should take 2–4 hours, not a week.

### 1. Scope (Clause 4.3)
- [ ] Documented scope of the QMS is current.
- [ ] Scope covers products/services, locations, and exclusions with justification.
- [ ] Scope is communicated to relevant interested parties.
- [ ] Scope statement is reviewed whenever you add a service line or enter a new market.

### 2. Interested parties (Clause 4.2)
- [ ] Relevant parties are identified and documented.
- [ ] Requirements are reviewed on a defined cadence.
- [ ] Monitoring plan includes regulatory, customer, and internal requirements.
- [ ] Changes in customer requirements trigger a scope review.

### 3. Risk-based thinking (Clause 6.1)
- [ ] Risks and opportunities are identified for each clause/process.
- [ ] Actions are proportionate to impact.
- [ ] Residual risk is recorded and reviewed.
- [ ] Mitigations include both preventive action and contingency plans.

### 4. Leadership and commitment (Clause 5.1)
- [ ] Quality policy is visible to the team.
- [ ] Quality objectives are set and communicated.
- [ ] Management review minutes show evidence of leadership involvement.

### 5. Support and resources (Clause 7.1)
- [ ] Competence requirements are defined for key roles.
- [ ] Training records are kept up to date.
- [ ] Infrastructure and environment for process operation is adequate.

### 6. Operational controls (Clause 8.1)
- [ ] Documented information is available at the point of use.
- [ ] Process criteria are defined and controlled.
- [ ] Non-conformity handling is consistent with Clause 10.
- [ ] Design controls cover service or product changes.

### 7. Performance evaluation (Clause 9)
- [ ] Internal audits are scheduled and evidence-based.
- [ ] Management reviews happen at planned intervals.
- [ ] Monitoring and measurement methods are defined and calibrated.

### 8. Improvement (Clause 10)
- [ ] Corrective actions include root-cause analysis.
- [ ] Effectiveness of actions is verified before closure.
- [ ] Lessons learned are captured and reused.

### Common pitfalls for small teams
- Skipping the interested-party review because “we know our customers.”
- Writing the QMS only for certification, not for daily use.
- Treating risk-based thinking as a one-time spreadsheet exercise.
