## NCR tracking best practices that actually close the loop

Why most NCR registers become paper trails, and a lightweight workflow for root cause, action planning, and effectiveness verification.

### The problem
- Finding logged, no owner assigned.
- Action planned, no due date.
- Closed without evidence.

### A better workflow
1. **Raise** — capture clause reference, severity, and evidence source immediately.
2. **Assign** — single owner + target date + verification method.
3. **Review** — scheduled effectiveness check with documented outcome.
4. **Close** — approval, evidence attachment, timestamp.

### Checklist
- [ ] Severity classification is applied at creation.
- [ ] Owner and due date are mandatory before status can move out of Open.
- [ ] Effectiveness verification is recorded before transition to Closed.
